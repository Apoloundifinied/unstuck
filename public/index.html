06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Some things
up front.

Can I hire someone today?

Not through Unstuck yet. This is an early-access waitlist. The directory and contracting experience are still being validated.

Does the waitlist cost anything?

No. Joining is free. Marketplace pricing has not been decided.

Is this a template marketplace?

No. The initial focus is connecting people to independent n8n specialists for custom builds, integrations, fixes and maintenance.

Will you access my n8n instance?

No. The waitlist does not connect to n8n or ask for workflow files, access tokens or credentials.

What information do you collect?

Email, whether you're looking for help or offering it, optional qualification answers, and basic attribution. See the privacy notice.
